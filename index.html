Peel Talk — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

What this document covers

This policy covers the Peel Talk mobile application ("the app") available on iOS and Android.

What we collect

Analytics events (PostHog)

We use PostHog to collect anonymous usage data. PostHog assigns a random identifier to each installation — it is not linked to your name, email, or any account. We never collect or store personally identifiable information.

Every event carries only the fields listed above. No message content, no card text, no free-form input from users is ever recorded.

Device information (automatic, via PostHog)

PostHog automatically captures:

  • Device type (for example, mobile)
  • Screen dimensions

No advertising identifier (IDFA/GAID) is read or transmitted.

What we do not collect

  • No name, email, phone number, or any account credentials — the app has no accounts
  • No location data
  • No contacts or address book
  • No camera or microphone access
  • No advertising identifier
  • No payment information (handled entirely by the platform app store)
  • No card text or session content

How data is used

Analytics data is used solely to understand how the app is used in aggregate — which features are used, where sessions typically end, how settings are configured. This informs which cards and features to develop next.

We do not use this data for advertising, and we do not sell it.

Data hosting and retention

PostHog data is stored on servers in the European Union. Data is retained for 12 months then deleted automatically.

Third parties

The only third-party data processor is PostHog, Inc. Their privacy policy is at posthog.com/privacy.

No advertising networks, social SDKs, or analytics tools beyond PostHog are used.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 (US) or under 16 (EU). We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Your rights

Because no personal data is linked to you, there is no account to delete and no profile to export. The anonymous PostHog identifier is stored only on your device. You can reset it at any time by clearing the app's data in your device settings (Settings → Apps → Peel Talk → Storage → Clear Data on Android; or deleting and reinstalling the app on iOS).

If you have questions about data practices, contact: n.kuzmit@proton.me

Changes to this policy

If material changes are made, the "Last updated" date at the top of this document will be updated. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance.

Peel Talk is developed independently. This policy applies only to the Peel Talk app.
